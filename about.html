About

The person behind the pixels

I'm Ravi Sharma, a brand and product designer based in Bengaluru, India, with over eight years of experience helping startups and studios build memorable visual identities and digital products.

My process blends strategy and craft: I start by understanding a brand's audience and goals, then move quickly into sketches, prototypes, and high-fidelity design so ideas can be tested and refined together with my clients.

When I'm not designing, you'll find me experimenting with letterpress printing, hiking in the Western Ghats, or mentoring new designers through local design meetups.
